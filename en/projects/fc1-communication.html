FC1: compute–communication contention

Jointly tune NCCL CTA and DeepGEMM SM budgets in a dual-stream, four-H200 proxy.

Project indexIQuest-Q1

Problem & contribution

Communication on a separate stream competed with FC1 for GPU resources, degrading concurrent compute performance.

Built a dual-stream proxy on four H200 GPUs and co-tuned NCCL CTA and DeepGEMM SM budgets. Ran 121 coarse configurations, 89 fine configurations and rechecks; held communication settings fixed to isolate the compute-budget contribution.
