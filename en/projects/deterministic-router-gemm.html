Deterministic MoE router GEMM

Three backends for small-M decode with CUDA Graph preflight and bitwise checks.

Project indexIQuest-Q1

Problem & contribution

Small-M decode wasted GEMM work while kernel selection and CUDA Graph execution had to satisfy determinism constraints.

Integrated DeepGEMM, Triton Full-K and persistent backends into vLLM. Implemented dispatch, Graph preflight, cache/fallback and workspace management, then validated kernel and model scenarios.
