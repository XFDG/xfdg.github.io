FA3: deterministic SWA backward scheduling

Shorten the dQ semaphore dependency chain inside the fused kernel.

Project indexIQuest-Q1

Problem & contribution

Long-sequence sliding-window attention regressed in deterministic backward mode. The goal was lower backward latency without losing bitwise determinism.

Owned diagnosis, scheduling changes and validation. Nsys / NCU isolated the dQ semaphore dependency chain; contributor-relative tickets were implemented in the existing fused kernel and aligned with the reverse scheduler.
