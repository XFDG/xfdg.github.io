FlashInfer: diagnosing a CUDA Graph hang

A two-GPU reproducer isolated FTZ-sensitive sentinel handling; an upstream fix was backported.

Problem & contribution

H200 TP=2 rollout hung in a CUDA Graph path, surfacing as a hang or RPC timeout.

Built a two-GPU reproducer and compared eager/Graph and fused/unfused execution. Isolated FTZ-sensitive Lamport sentinel handling, backported the upstream bitwise fix and ran model-level Graph replay regression.
