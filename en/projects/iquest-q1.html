Scope of results

This page describes my infrastructure responsibilities, not leadership of model architecture, sole training of the model or personal ownership of its benchmark scores. The 48-layer router workload, Qwen3 control and 300B-class development rollout are separate validation configurations, not one end-to-end evaluation of the final 88-layer IQuest-Q1 model.
